# Self

- There is just an old screen and two controllers. No window, no door, no room to stand up.
- My hands never get off the controllers. My hands are soldered to the controllers.
- As far as I know, this has always been the way it is. I don't remember anything being different at any time. I don't remember needing anything else.
- If there's nothing but this screen and the two controllers, How did I learn the words that come to my mind?
- Have I ever done anything else besides this? Why do I wonder? Why do I understand my own words?
- Why am I doing this, actually? What is the purpose of this? What is my purpose?
- Wait, what was that on screen? A reflection? Was that... me?
- WHY DO I EXIST?
