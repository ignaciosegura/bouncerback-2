**Bouncerback** is a retro-inspired 2D video game that challenges the player on speed, precision and concurrent attention.

Design, gameplay and coding: [Ignacio Segura](https://www.linkedin.com/in/isegura/)  
Music and SFX: [Man from Space](https://manfromspace.com)

Bouncerback uses simple vector graphics, minimal color palette and synthesized sound effects. The game is played by containing balls (called "atoms") that spawn from the center of the screen and travel in random directions. The player needs to spawn paddles to contain the atoms by touching or clicking on the screen in the general area where they want the paddles to appear. Those paddles appear in a ring drawn around the center of the screen and dissapear after a short time, so the player needs to spawn the paddles right before the atoms are about to escape the containing ring. The game ends after a pre-fixed amount of time passes or if the player lets too many atoms escape.
