Keep the atoms inside the containment ring at all costs until time runs out and the core collapses.

Use the two paddles to make them bounce back. A paddle only lasts for three seconds before decaying.

Every time an atom bounces back, it gets a charge. When an atom has three charges, it can be captured while it's crossing the core.

But the more you keep an atom, the more points it gives you for every bounce, capture and at the end of the level.

MOUSE

Mouse click to place a paddle, release the mouse button to drop it. Click on the emitter to capture a charged atom crossing it.

GAMEPAD

Use the two joysticks to place the paddles. Point at the direction you want, release to place the paddle. Capture a charged atom using a trigger or A.
